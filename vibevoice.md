# vibevoice

> Copia/fork di Microsoft VibeVoice, TTS open source.

## Concetti
[[LLM]] [[Python]]

## Panoramica
VibeVoice: voice AI open source, incluso **VibeVoice-Realtime-0.5B** (TTS streaming con input testuale e generazione lunga). Speaker sperimentali in 9 lingue (incluso IT) e 11 stili inglesi. Il repo contiene `vibevoice/`, `demo/`, `docs/`, `Figures/`.

## Tech Stack
Python (PyTorch). Origine: https://github.com/microsoft/VibeVoice. Repo: https://github.com/pakkio/vibevoice
